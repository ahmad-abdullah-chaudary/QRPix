Privacy First

QRPix is designed as a client-side application. Your data stays on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or sell your personal information.

1. Introduction

Welcome to QRPix ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application QRPix (the "App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our practices.

By using QRPix, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information Processed Locally

The following information is processed entirely on your device and is never transmitted to our servers or any third party:

  • Camera data — Camera feed used for QR code scanning is processed in real-time and is not recorded or stored.
  • Images you select — Photos chosen for "Logo in QR" processing remain on your device.
  • QR code content — URLs, text, Wi-Fi credentials, and other data from scanned or generated QR codes.
  • Generated QR images — QR codes you create are stored locally on your device.
  • Scan history — Records of your scans are stored in a local SQLite database on your device only.

2.2 Information Collected by Third-Party Services

QRPix uses Google Mobile Ads for monetization. Google AdMob may collect:

  • Advertising ID — A unique, user-resettable identifier for ad personalization.
  • Device information — Device model, operating system version, language settings.
  • App usage data — Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks).

This data is collected by Google and is subject to Google's Privacy Policy. We do not have access to or control over this data.

3. How We Use Information

Since QRPix is primarily a client-side app, we do not use your personal information for any purpose. The App processes all data locally on your device.

Third-party ad data is used by Google to serve relevant advertisements and is governed by Google's own policies.

4. Data Storage and Security

  • Local storage only: All scan history, generated QR codes, and images are stored in your device's local storage using SQLite database and the application's private directory.
  • No cloud storage: We do not upload or store any of your data on remote servers.
  • Data deletion: You can delete your data at any time through Settings > Clear All History. Uninstalling the App will also remove all local data.
  • No encryption needed: Since data never leaves your device, no additional encryption is required for data in transit.

5. Camera Permission

QRPix requires camera permission to scan QR codes and barcodes. The camera feed is processed in real-time using on-device scanning technology. No images or video from the camera are recorded, stored, or transmitted.

6. Location Permission

QRPix may request location permission for Wi-Fi connection features. This permission is used solely to connect to Wi-Fi networks when you scan a Wi-Fi QR code and tap "Connect." Your location data is not stored or transmitted.

7. Children's Privacy

QRPix is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

8. Third-Party Services

QRPix uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob — For displaying advertisements. Learn more
  • Google Mobile Ads SDK — For ad mediation and delivery.

Each third-party service has its own privacy policy governing the use of your information.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights:

  • Right to access — View the data stored locally in the App.
  • Right to deletion — Delete your data via Settings or by uninstalling the App.
  • Right to opt-out — Opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page with an updated effective date. You are advised to review this policy periodically.
