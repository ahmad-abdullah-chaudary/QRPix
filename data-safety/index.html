Data Safety

How QRPix handles your data — Last updated September 21, 2026

Key Takeaway

QRPix is a client-side app. Your scans, images, and generated QR codes never leave your device. The only data that leaves your device is anonymized ad delivery data handled by Google.

How Your Data Is Protected

On-Device Processing

  • All QR scanning uses your device's camera directly — no video is recorded or uploaded
  • All image processing for "Logo in QR" happens in your device's memory
  • All QR code generation is performed locally
  • Scan history is stored in an encrypted SQLite database on your device

No Account Required

  • QRPix requires no sign-up, login, or account creation
  • No email, name, or personal identifiers are collected
  • No user profiles are created or maintained

Data You Control

  • You can clear all history at any time via Settings > Clear All History
  • You can control whether scan history is saved via Settings > Save Scan History
  • You can set auto-deletion periods (30, 60, 90, 180, 365 days, or Never)
  • Uninstalling the App permanently deletes all local data

Children's Privacy

QRPix does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The App does not require account creation and does not collect personal information from any user.
